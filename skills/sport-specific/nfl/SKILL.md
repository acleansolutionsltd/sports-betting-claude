# SKILL: NFL Context

> **Purpose:** Sport-specific knowledge that adjusts edge calculations for NFL-specific factors the base model doesn't capture.

## Key edge sources in NFL betting

### 1. Quarterback injuries (highest impact in sports)
- Starting QB out → reduce team to -15 to -20 points depending on backup quality
- Backup quality tiers:
  - Proven starter: -5 to -8 points
  - Career backup with starts: -10 to -13 points
  - Practice squad call-up: -18 to -22 points
- **Always confirm QB status** before betting any NFL game

### 2. Offensive line integrity
- OL is underweighted by public; sharps attack it heavily
- Missing 2+ OL starters against elite pass rushers → -3 to -5 points
- Check: starting RT/LT/LG matchup vs opponent's top pass rusher

### 3. Divisional game reduction
- Familiarity reduces betting edges; reduce spread by 20-25%
- Example: 6-point favorite in a non-division game → treat as 4.5–5 in division
- Second matchup in same season (rematches): reduce further — defense has game film

### 4. Situational spots
Identify these before every game:

**Trap game:**
- Team coming off huge win against elite opponent
- Facing a clearly inferior but trappy opponent
- Next week: another elite opponent (look-ahead risk)
- Lean: against the trap team (especially if heavy favorite)

**Revenge spot:**
- Team facing a team that beat them badly last season or earlier this year
- Especially if a key player was injured in that game
- Lean: slight favor to revenge team

**Primetime spots:**
- Teams with poor primetime records (MNF/SNF/TNF)
- Some teams consistently underperform under lights — check historical record
- Short-week games (Thursday Night Football): rest and injury impacts amplified

**Bye week advantage:**
- Team coming off bye: +1.5 to +2.5 points on spread
- Team playing team coming off bye: slight disadvantage

### 5. Weather (totals only)
- Wind > 15mph: reduce total by 1.5–2.5 points; lean under
- Precipitation: -0.5 to -1.0 on total
- Cold temps below 25°F: reduce total by 1–1.5 points
- Dome teams playing outdoors in bad weather: additional -2 to -3 points

### 6. DVOA context
- Reference Football Outsiders DVOA (Defense-adjusted Value Over Average)
- Search: `Football Outsiders DVOA current week`
- High-DVOA offense vs low-DVOA defense: edge exists even if line looks right
- Specifically: passing DVOA differential is strongest predictor

### 7. Pace and totals
- Calculate: (Team A plays/game + Team B plays/game) / 2 vs league average
- Fast pace teams: lean over on totals
- Search: `[team] pace of play [current season]`

### 8. Public bias patterns
- Public loves: favorites, home teams, overs, prime time games, winning teams
- Books shade lines 0.5–1 point to account for public bias on heavily bet games
- Contrarian value exists when 70%+ public on one side and line doesn't move toward them

## NFL-specific red flags (lean to pass)

```
❌ QB status not confirmed by Thursday (for Sunday games) — wait or pass
❌ Heavy public underdog + line moving against you (double public play)
❌ Revenge spot for the team you're picking against (they have motivation)
❌ Team on short week with injury to key skill position
❌ Divisional game where underdog has won last 3 meetings
❌ Total hasn't moved despite major injury news — sharp books disagree
```

## Bet type preferences by situation

| Situation | Preferred bet type |
|---|---|
| Key injury news dropped today | Spread or ML (adjusts fastest) |
| Strong weather forecast | Total (over/under), lean under |
| Divisional game, close line | ML on underdog (reduce spread risk) |
| Blowout candidate (elite vs cellar) | First half spread (reduces garbage time noise) |
| High-total game | First half over (prevents late game script affects) |
| Public darling | Fade via ATS underdog |
